Setting up your morning dashboard

One page you open before the market: how the account is doing, what changed overnight, and what needs attention today. You keep trading in thinkorswim. The dashboard only reads and can't place trades.

Open the dashboard ↗

Setup takes about 15 minutes. Step 1 works today with nothing to install.

  1. 1
    Today · 5 minutes

    Load your Schwab numbers

    Claude has no connector for personal Schwab accounts yet, so the dashboard starts with two files you export from Schwab.com.

    1. Positions: Accounts → Positions → Export (the arrow icon, top right).
    2. Transactions: Accounts → History → set the date range from January 1 to today → Export.
    3. Open the dashboard and drop both files on the Drop Schwab CSVs here box.
    4. Type your January 1 account value (the ending balance on your December statement) so the year's return is right.

    A checklist then compares the dashboard with Schwab's own totals. A green check means it matches. Anything amber tells you what to check. The files stay in your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.

  2. 2
    10 minutes · in your Claude account

    Connect your apps to Claude

    Go to claude.ai/customize/connectors, search for each app below and sign in. Each one uses your own login, and you can disconnect it any time from the same page.

  3. 3
    Reply when you have a minute

    Tell us how you like it set up

    A few preferences, so the brief and alerts are about your book and not generic market noise:

    • Which Gmail senders count (Schwab, your alert service, others)
    • Newsletters you want in the morning brief
    • Your watchlist tickers
    • News sites and blogs you read
    • Which Google calendar holds your trips
    • Your actual weekly workout plan
    • Time you usually open it (we have it compiling by 6:45 AM ET)
    • Anything you look at every morning that isn't on the page

    Please don't send account numbers, passwords or statements by email. Nothing here needs them.

  4. 4
    Next · we handle this

    Automatic Schwab updates

    The last step replaces the CSV exports with a read-only connection through Schwab's Trader API, so balances, positions, orders and trades refresh on their own every morning. Schwab asks you to sign in again about once a week to keep it active, and the dashboard will remind you when that's due. Before relying on the full-year numbers, we'll confirm how much trade history Schwab makes available.
